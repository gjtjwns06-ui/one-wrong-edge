# Source and artifact notices

The research corpus is [Metamath set.mm](https://github.com/metamath/set.mm), released under the **CC0 1.0 Universal** dedication. Its original license, source revision, URLs and file hashes are preserved in [study/data/raw/LICENSE](study/data/raw/LICENSE) and [study/data/raw/manifest.json](study/data/raw/manifest.json). The CC0 label in the README refers to this source corpus.

This publication keeps the original research code, reports and evidence unchanged under `study/`. Git carries a compact selection; the complete original snapshot, including models and measurements, is provided as a release asset. [PUBLICATION.json](PUBLICATION.json) records exact hashes and the source of each figure used on the landing page.

The presentation banner is a conceptual illustration. The result figures are copied directly from the completed research runs. Historical figures are labeled by study version; results across different test sets are not treated as before/after measurements.
